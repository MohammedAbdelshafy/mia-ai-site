WORKFLOW PLANNING

Make a repeatable task easier to follow.

This page explores a way to map work that is usually split across tabs, spreadsheets and messages. The public demo is illustrative; it does not run connected automations on its own.

Explore the Mia preview →

Lead & outreach workflows

Possible workflow: research approved business sources, draft a tailored note and plan the follow-up. Sending or changing records needs the right permissions and human review.

Content pipelines

Possible workflow: research a topic and prepare drafts or assets for review. Publishing remains a separate approved step.

Reporting workflows

Possible workflow: summarize supplied data, show its sources and flag items that need review.

Build & delivery

Possible workflow: write a specification, use the connected implementation tools and check the result before handoff.

Best starting point: choose one task that repeats each week. Define the current steps and one measure of success before automating anything.
