## Positioning

**Mia gives AI a face, voice, and visible workflow.** The public website is a cinematic, interactive preview of an embodied AI operator. The public chat and mission animations are illustrative and do not run external tools. Offer live installation work only when the customer’s stack, integrations, and acceptance criteria have been confirmed.

## Best first offer: Embodied AI Design-Partner Pilot

**Suggested pilot price: $499 fixed-scope implementation** (founding offer, subject to confirming scope on a discovery call).

Deliverables:
- 45-minute discovery and workflow mapping.
- One responsive Mia-style assistant experience for a single website or product.
- Customer-branded character/presence layer using client-provided or licensed assets.
- One agreed interaction flow (greeting, guided task intake, sample workflow, lead/demo capture).
- Basic event tracking and form handoff into one confirmed destination.
- Deployment support to the customer's existing hosting environment.
- Handover notes and a short recorded walkthrough.

Exclusions unless separately scoped: full autonomous agent execution, arbitrary desktop control, ongoing video generation, phone calls, custom voice cloning, SSO, multi-tenant administration, production-grade agent infrastructure, and paid third-party usage.

Acceptance: responsive design; approved branding; one interaction flow; form delivery demonstrated end-to-end; no false claims about actions that were not executed.

Payment: use a live checkout or invoice link that the founder has tested before sending. Do not imply that a payment option is available until confirmed.

3. Product studios building vertical AI tools and customer portals.
4. B2B software teams testing a guided AI onboarding or in-product copilot.

## Discovery call questions

1. What AI experience exists today, and where do users get stuck?
2. What is the one task the assistant should help complete?
3. Which model/API and hosting environment are already in place?
4. Which tools must it call, and which actions require user approval?
5. What data is collected and where must it be stored?
6. What measurable outcome would make a first pilot successful?
7. Who owns the integration and approves deployment?

## Sales funnel

1. Publish the site and short demo clips.
2. Invite interested founders to a 15-minute discovery call.
3. Qualify for a single workflow and confirmed integration path.
4. Send a written $499 scope with exclusions, acceptance criteria, and delivery milestones.
5. Collect payment only through a tested checkout/invoice.
6. Deliver one workflow and ask permission to publish a case study.
7. Offer additional workflows or support only after the pilot succeeds.

## Proof and honesty rules

- Describe mission videos as illustrative visuals, not evidence of live execution.
- Say “57 specialist profiles” or “specialist directory” unless each agent is connected to a verified capability.
- Never claim that outreach, browsing, coding, or deployment happened unless it did.
- Do not claim customer results, testimonials, waitlist counts, or revenue without evidence.
- Avoid mass spam. Personalize messages to relevant business context, include opt-out language where appropriate, and observe the recipient's rules and applicable law.
